Animal Mysteries Privacy Policy

Animal Mysteries uses Google OAuth for authorization. The application requests YouTube authorization only as necessary to upload videos to the authorized YouTube account.

Google authentication credentials and tokens are used for authentication. They are not sold or shared with third parties for advertising.

The application is currently used privately by its owner and is not offered as a public service.

Google Account access for Animal Mysteries can be revoked through the connected apps and services section of the user's Google Account security settings.
